# A Tunnel to Summer, the Exit of Goodbyes

**Plot**
*The story is set in a fictional Japanese city near Kasaki Station, where an urban legend speaks of the "Urashima Tunnel", a mysterious passage said to grant wishes—though at a cost. Kaoru Touno, a reclusive high school student in a rural town, commutes daily on the Oosara-Sugimori train line.*

**Characters**
*Kaoru Touno*
*Anzu Hanashiro*
*Shouhei Kaga*
*Koharu Kawasaki*
*Hanamoto-sensei*
*Kaoru's father*
*Karen Touno*
